## About (раздел «Общие сведения»)

I run MORE Group, a real estate advisory in Phuket, and the eleven content sites that bring it clients from Google and, since 2026, from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot.

Building those sites taught me what breaks quietly at scale: titles cut mid-phrase, paragraphs copied across a hundred pages with the numbers swapped, an AI index that advertises pages that no longer exist. None of it fails a build. All of it costs leads.

OperStack is the tooling I wrote to stop it, packaged so one person can run it:
• Fifteen gates: automated quality checks for MDX content sites, free (npx @operstack/gates)
• An Astro starter that passes its own gates, free
• A Claude Code plugin for writing pages that answer engines quote, free
• An SEO, AEO and GEO audit with a fix plan, and a Site Kit for launching a 300-page site

If you publish at scale and want to know whether AI assistants can read your site, the free check is at oper-stack.com/ai-visibility.
